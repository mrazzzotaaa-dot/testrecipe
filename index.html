CINNAMOROLL

>This traditional and easy recipe makes fluffy, golden pancakes that are perfect for a quick breakfast or a sweet weekend treat.

Ingredients

  • Wheat flour — 200 g
  • Chicken eggs — 2 pcs
  • Milk — 300 ml
  • Sugar — 1 tablespoon

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and sugar.
  2. Add the eggs and slowly pour in the milk while whisking continuously until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat.
  4. Pour the batter onto the skillet and cook until golden brown on both sides.
